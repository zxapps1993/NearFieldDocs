Nearfield Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

ZXApps provides Nearfield for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. You can use Nearfield without creating a Nearfield account. ZXApps does not receive the files you transfer, use in-app analytics or advertising trackers, or sell personal data.

Nearby discovery and transfers

When discovery is active, Nearfield uses Bonjour on your local network. It advertises the device name supplied by your operating system, an app-generated discovery identifier, the device type, and supported transfer capabilities. Other devices on the same local network may see this discovery information before you start a transfer.

Nearfield sends the content you select directly to the device or devices you choose. The receiving device receives file names, sizes, and content as needed for the transfer. Nearfield encrypts transfers in transit and asks you to confirm a matching code. The recipient can keep or share a received copy. Removing a file from your device does not remove copies held by a recipient.

What Nearfield stores on your device

  • App preferences and the discovery identifier, so the app can remember your settings and recognize its own discovery messages.
  • Transfer receipts, which can include file names, sizes, dates, the other device's name, transfer status, and saved file locations. Nearfield also keeps temporary imports and transfer checkpoints to queue or resume transfers. When needed, it stores access references for files or folders you selected.
  • A free outgoing transfer usage counter in Apple Keychain. The counter can remain on the device after you uninstall Nearfield.

The app checks metadata for files and folders it uses, and checks available storage before saving incoming files. The iPhone and iPad Share extension places copies of selected items into storage shared with the Nearfield app on that device.

Permissions, retention, and deletion

You can pause nearby discovery in Nearfield Settings and manage local network and notification permissions in your device settings where available. If you allow notifications, Nearfield schedules transfer alerts on your device. Nearfield accesses the files and media you choose through the file picker, Photos picker, drag and drop, or Share menu.

You can remove queued copies from Shared Items and delete received files from their saved locations using Files or Finder. Transfer receipts stay in Nearfield's local app data. To remove that data, delete the app on iPhone or iPad, or remove its local app data on Mac. Files saved outside the app's storage, copies on other devices, backups, and Keychain items can remain separately. Partial files or resume data can also remain in a folder you chose for a transfer.

App Store purchases

Apple processes payments and provides Nearfield with StoreKit product and transaction information to verify purchases, manage access, and restore eligible purchases. ZXApps does not receive your payment card details. Apple's handling of this information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.
